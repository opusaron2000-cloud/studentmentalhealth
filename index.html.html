About Us

The Student Wellness Centre website is designed to provide students with information about mental
health, counselling services and available support.

Our Purpose

  • To increase mental health awareness among students.
  • To provide educational mental health information.
  • To guide students on accessing counselling services.
  • To encourage participation in wellness activities.
  • To help students find appropriate support resources.

Our Target Audience

Our main target audience is university students who
need mental health information, counselling guidance
and opportunities to participate in wellness activities.
